01 / Business thinking

The Marketing Challenge

Attention is easy to count. Brand meaning is harder to understand.

The project moved beyond reporting mentions. We framed the campaign around what drove participation, how consumers felt, and how Doritos could turn a short burst of attention into stronger brand value.

Can a revived UGC campaign still win in a fragmented, algorithm-driven media environment?

Doritos brought back a well-known crowdsourced advertising format. Our task was to evaluate whether nostalgia, humor and public voting still created meaningful consumer engagement.

06 / Strategic thinking

If I Managed the Next Campaign

I would turn a one-time advertising event into a longer consumer relationship.

Each recommendation responds directly to a gap found in the analysis: channel concentration, attention decline after voting and weak linkage between campaign buzz and product purchase.

01 / REACH

Expand short-form video

Use TikTok and YouTube Shorts, creator participation, filters and AR effects to make campaign creation native to younger audiences.

WHY: reduce over-reliance on X
02 / RETENTION

Design multi-stage participation

Extend attention through finalist rounds, live reveals, real-time rankings and voting incentives instead of ending the conversation after one vote.

WHY: sustain momentum after campaign peaks
03 / CONVERSION

Bring the product back

Connect winning creative to limited-edition flavors, packaging and product-centered UGC so advertising attention supports trial and loyalty.

WHY: turn ad discussion into brand value
